## 1. Scoring and response

### 1.1 Score

- **Probability:** `1` Rare, `2` Unlikely, `3` Possible, `4` Likely, `5` Almost certain.
- **Impact:** `1` Negligible, `2` Minor, `3` Moderate, `4` Major, `5` Severe.
- **Score:** Probability multiplied by Impact.
- **Priority:** Low `1-4`, Medium `5-9`, High `10-14`, Critical `15-25`.

### 1.2 Priority response

| Priority | Required response |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | Immediate role-based escalation, named mitigation owner, phase or release stop when the trigger is met, and explicit acceptance by Technology and Product before proceeding |
| High | Mitigation and contingency must be tested before the affected phase exits; escalation occurs if probability, impact, or trigger worsens |
| Medium | Track with an owner and due date; include in phase review and prioritize against capacity |
| Low | Maintain the mitigation and review at the normal cadence; escalate if conditions change |

### 1.3 Risk statement rule

Each statement follows this form:

> Because a specific cause exists, a specific failure may occur, which will affect a specific product, operational, security, financial, legal, or customer outcome.

## 4. Escalation rules

- **Critical risk:** Notify the named owner, Product Owner, Technology Owner, and affected operations or security role immediately. A release or phase cannot continue on the trigger path without an explicit, documented risk decision.
- **High risk:** Add the item to the phase exit review, name a due date and contingency test, and escalate if probability or impact rises by one level.
- **Security, privacy, payment, or tenant isolation:** Escalate regardless of calculated score when credible evidence of exposure, false access grant, unauthorized activation, data loss, or incorrect financial state exists.
- **New risk:** Assign the next unused `KROWDS-RISK-###` identifier, owner role, score, mitigation, trigger, and review date during the next review or immediately when Critical.
- **Risk closure:** Closure requires evidence that the cause is removed or accepted, no residual trigger remains, and the accountable role approves the closure date.

## 5. Review method

At each review, owners shall:

1. Re-score probability and impact using current evidence.
2. Confirm preventive controls are operating rather than merely planned.
3. Exercise or schedule contingency actions for Critical and High risks.
4. Check roadmap dependencies, test results, open decisions, incidents, vendor notices, and cost or capacity signals.
5. Add newly discovered risks without weakening existing controls to close an older item.
6. Record score changes, accepted residual risk, new controls, and next action in the review log.
